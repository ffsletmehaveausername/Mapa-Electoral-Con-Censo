CENSUS AVAILABILITY INVENTORY - PUERTO RICO
Research date: November 12, 2026
Requested years: 2008, 2012, 2016, 2020, 2024

SUMMARY

The Census API key works. API metadata and official TIGER directory
listings were checked and compared with files in this workspace. The
2012 backfill has since added the official ACS 2008-2012 Summary File
inputs and TIGER 2012 Puerto Rico block-group shapefile, and generated
PREKEY and UNIKEY estimates. These public files do not require the API key.

"Missing" below means the matching Census vintage is absent from this
workspace. It does not mean you lack a general municipio basemap.

For electoral analysis, municipio-level ACS data is the simplest complete
geographic comparison: Puerto Rico municipios are Census county-equivalent
geographies. The local map already has a municipio basemap, but its boundary
vintage is not documented here. Use municipio GEOIDs and verify the geometry
vintage rather than assuming boundary files are identical across years.

links is marked unverified, not treated as proof that no files exist.

IMPORTANT:
  An ACS five-year label is the END year of a five-year estimate period.
  A TIGER vintage describes geographic boundaries, not the observation
  period of the statistics. Match geographic identifiers and applicable
  boundary definitions before joining data to a map.

4. RECOMMENDED ANALYSIS DESIGN AND NEXT ADDITIONS

Use three complementary geography levels rather than choosing only one:

  1. Municipio: join municipio-level ACS estimates to electoral results
     aggregated to municipio. This is the clearest full-coverage comparison
     and can include returns from non-geographic voting units through their
     precinct/municipio totals.
  2. PREKEY / precinct: retain precinct-level election results and estimate
     socioeconomic values by overlaying block groups with precinct polygons.
     Precincts vary substantially in size, so some estimates will be coarse.
  3. PREKEY+UNIKEY / voting unit: retain unit-level results and estimate
     socioeconomic values for units with polygons. Non-geographic units
     remain in the election data but cannot receive a spatial SES estimate.

Municipio results provide a stable, directly joined baseline; PREKEY and
UNIKEY add finer spatial detail through areal estimation. They are estimates,
not direct ACS observations for those electoral areas. Keep boundary
vintages and municipality GEOIDs explicit, and compare levels as distinct
geographies rather than expecting their estimates to add up exactly.

  1. 2008 block-group boundaries, if finer maps for that year are needed.
  2. Census tracts for requested vintages, if tract-level analysis is useful.
  3. Missing socioeconomic topics listed above.

For 2008, block-group boundaries can be displayed, but the absence of a
2008 ACS five-year release prevents a comparable ACS block-group data
layer for that year.

For 2008, the available one- and three-year ACS products cover only 12
and 65 municipios, respectively; they do not provide a complete comparable
municipio layer. Municipio-level five-year estimates cover all 78 municipios
for 2012, 2016, 2020, and 2024.

For 2012, block-group statistics require the separate Summary File workflow
implemented in scripts/build_2012.py. It generated comparable estimates for 1,877
variables; 155 unavailable variables are listed in
outputs/2012/omitted_variables.csv. Its 2008-2012 ACS period overlaps the
2012 election year and is a period-context association, not a causal measure.

The remaining items are analysis recommendations; no additional bulk
download was started for them.
